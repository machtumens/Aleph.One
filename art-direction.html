Original recommendation, superseded by Direction 4

Phosphor.

Against §1. The logo already lives in this world: white mark, cyan halo, near-black. Phosphor makes the site the room the logo was photographed in. Cyanotype asks the brand to change blues; Ledger asks it to change everything. Premium means intentional, and the most intentional move is to take what exists and hold it.

Against §2. Visitor A on a 4G Android gets #EDEFF0 on #070809 — the highest-contrast, lowest-power combination on an OLED screen — with one cyan number pointing at the price. Poster tier needs no 3D to feel finished; the type carries it. Visitor B gets the same dark room with the light turned on: emissive edges on transmission planes are the one place the GPU budget goes.

The blue. #35D6F0, sampled from the halo, then pulled toward P3 cyan. It is not Tailwind blue-500 and not a "tech" indigo. Referent: phosphor P22 green-blue on a CRT — light a screen makes, which is what the studio sells. Used for selected state, active nav, the price, and nothing structural.

Why Archivo, given the warning. Not on the default AI stack. Width axis 62–125 gives a state change to animate (a service row widening on hover; the selected tier expanding in the configurator) without touching weight. Holds at 10rem with tight tracking; at width 88 the Indonesian compounds fit ≤72 characters without hyphenation. Instrument Sans for body because it disappears, which is the job.

What Phosphor rules out. No gradients — the glow is a box-shadow on one edge, once per surface. No cards; surfaces are #0E1012 regions separated by hairlines. Cyan never fills a large area. Ambient grain and glow exist only in Cinema tier.

Flag. The wordmark reads "aleph.one solutions". §12 bans "solusi" in copy; the descriptor stays in the logo asset but the site never says it in prose. Confirm you are fine with that tension or supply a wordmark-only lockup.
