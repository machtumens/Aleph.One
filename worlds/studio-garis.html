  2. Case study 01 / 06

    Rumah Kebun

    House · Salatiga, Central Java · 2018 · 214 m²

    A house for two retired teachers in an old mango and durian orchard on the lower slopes of Merbabu. We moved the plan twice to keep eleven trees. The walls are board-marked concrete, left as struck; the long window in the study sits at desk height, so the orchard reads as a horizon rather than a view.

02

Studio

A small practice by choice. We take on four projects a year and see each one through to the last site visit.

Eleven people in Bandung, around one long table: architects, a model maker and a site engineer.

  1. 2.1

    Survey before sketch.

    We walk the site at three times of day and draw every tree over three metres before we draw a wall. The plan takes what is left.

  2. 2.2

    One material, said plainly.

    Concrete, timber or stone, each where it does its work. Nothing clad to look like something else, nothing painted to hide a joint.

  3. 2.3

    Shade before glass.

    Deep roofs, louvres and courts do the cooling. Air-conditioning is the last thing we draw, and in four of these six buildings we never drew it.
