04 — Terms

Words you'll see on every card.

Indonesian property has its own vocabulary. Here is what ours means, in plain English, so nothing on a card is a surprise at the notary.

Kavlingplot
A surveyed piece of land subdivided to be sold on its own, usually with a road already cut to it.
SHMSertifikat Hak Milik
Full freehold title, the strongest there is. Only Indonesian citizens can hold it.
HGBHak Guna Bangunan
A right to build and use, granted for a fixed term (typically 30 years) and extendable. Common on developer land.
PBGPersetujuan Bangunan Gedung
The building approval that replaced the IMB. A house without one is harder to mortgage.
AJBAkta Jual Beli
The deed of sale, signed before a land deed official (PPAT). Our commission is due on that day, not before.
Hookcorner plot
A plot on a corner with two road frontages. More light, more parking, usually priced a little higher.
Siap bangunready to build
Cleared, levelled, with road access and power at the boundary. You could start foundations next week.
KPRKredit Pemilikan Rumah
A home mortgage. "KPR-ready" means the paperwork is in a state a bank will lend against.
