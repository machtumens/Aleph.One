IVAtelier

Nol means zero.

We started in 2019 with one cutting table above a fabric shop in Dago and no plan for seasons. The atelier is now eleven people: two cutters, six machinists, a presser and two of us at the drawing table. We buy short runs of wool crepe, washed linen and undyed cotton, cut every piece in the room and finish buttonholes by hand. Nothing is sewn more than a staircase away from where it was drawn.
